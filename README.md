# FREE-LAB.cam

### My Stuff. My Agency. My World.

**A mixed-reality playground for curiosity, creativity and cognitive agency.** FREE-LAB.cam brings the world around you into a personal spatial archive—and turns your surroundings into a place to think, move, play and observe your progress.

## The idea

It starts on an ordinary autumn day. Trees, clouds, curious dogs, people running, a fallen log. What if mixed reality helped us **engage more actively with that world**, instead of escaping it?

FREE-LAB.cam connects two complementary experiences:

| **MY STUFF — Explore your world** | **AGENCY — Explore what you can do** |
| --- | --- |
| Capture photographs, everyday objects and discoveries. | Play ten mixed-reality activities involving attention, shapes, numbers and movement. |
| Browse sources and object segments as spatial cards. | Point, pinch, draw, grab and interact with tracked hands or controllers. |
| Search with your voice; view 3D objects, cinematic galleries and a Sphere Mosaic. | Choose free play or structured challenges; view recorded performance and progress. |

**My Stuff is curiosity about our surroundings. Agency is curiosity about ourselves.** The pairing is a creative metaphor, not a literal claim about left- and right-brain hemispheres.

## What can you do?

- **Collect reality.** Organize a personal library of captures and detected/segmented objects, revisit them spatially, and explore supported 3D assets.
- **Play in mixed reality.** Ten Agency activities: Shape Garden, Number Flow, Hand Harmony, Spatial Frames, Shape Sort, Chain Lab, Cube Lab, Throw Lab, Five Note and Thumb String.
- **Use natural interaction.** Meta Quest 3 passthrough and WebXR, both hands and Touch controllers; create shapes, connect your fingers with colourful strings, sort, throw and build.
- **Discover your patterns.** View recorded sessions, elapsed play time, attempts, accuracy, input-hand differences and trends over time. Optional, consent-based AI commentary summarizes aggregate observations.
- **Return without a headset.** Access the personal collection, progress, and settings from desktop or mobile browser interfaces.
- **Share spatial experiences.** Public and private rooms provide experimental ways of sharing a spatial environment.

## Technology and Meta Quest capabilities

Built with **WebXR, WebGL, Three.js and JavaScript**, backed by **Python, Flask and SQLite**. The spatial experience uses **Meta Quest passthrough, hand tracking, and controller input**. GLB previews can use Blender or browser rendering; tutorials can stream from independently hosted HLS playlists. Optional configured AI and speech services extend generation and feedback.

The application is deployed to Ubuntu using tested, checksum-verified releases and a watcher-driven GitHub workflow. Technical details and commands are in the [developer guide](app/README.md).

## Research direction — and an important distinction

FREE-LAB.cam is an **experimental creative and interaction platform**, not a diagnostic or therapeutic product. Gameplay metrics describe recorded performance in specific tasks; they do **not** establish improvements in memory, intelligence, neuroplasticity or cognitive health. Any scientific conclusions would require careful research and validation.

The longer-term ambition is to collaborate with researchers in cognitive science and human-computer interaction to explore the reliability and usefulness of spatial interactions, attention tasks and longitudinal measurements.
